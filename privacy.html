Privacy Policy for Quiver

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Quiver (“the app”) is a puzzle game made by TipsyPixels (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information is collected when you use the app.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address or contacts. Your game progress (levels cleared, settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • information about your device and the app (model, operating system, language),
  • how you interact with ads (for example, views and taps).

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time using “privacy choices” on the app’s home screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

How Google uses this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

If you buy “remove ads”, the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. Google Play tells the app whether you own the purchase.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be at this address, with the date above changed.
